Supported Scam Categories

ScamShield AI classifies incoming vectors against 14 documented cyber fraud categories commonly active across India:

  • 1. Phishing attacks: Deceptive login portals and security alerts
  • 2. Fake KYC updates: Threatening bank account or SIM deactivation
  • 3. UPI payment fraud: PIN request to receive money / cashback traps
  • 4. Fake job offers: Paid YouTube liking or task-based WFH scams
  • 5. Loan scams: Zero-CIBIL instantaneous loan APK extortion
  • 6. Lottery and prize scams: Unearned KBC or celebrity contest awards
  • 7. Refund scams: Fake income tax or electricity refund portals
  • 8. Courier scams: India Post / FedEx parcel seized notification
  • 9. Bank impersonation: Lookalike domains mimicking major banks
  • 10. Government impersonation: Spoofed IT Department, CBI, or Police
  • 11. Digital arrest threats: Coercive video call arrest pretexts
  • 12. OTP & PIN harvesting: Direct credential extraction requests
  • 13. Malicious URLs: Raw IP hostnames and suspicious TLDs
  • 14. Fake invoice fraud: Altered supplier bank details & arithmetic discrepancies

Security Design & Privacy Policy

• Zero Execution & SSRF Immunity: URLs submitted for inspection are lexically analyzed without automated network navigation or server-side socket execution, eliminating Server-Side Request Forgery risks.

• Immediate File Sanitization: Uploaded images and PDF invoices are immediately purged from local disk storage once OCR and textual parsing complete.

• Credential Redaction: Extracted passwords, OTPs, and sensitive account details are redacted prior to database persistence and preview display.

• Calibrated Risk Engine: Classifier raw logits are never presented directly to the user as absolute probabilities; composite scoring incorporates structural heuristic evidence and severity weights.
